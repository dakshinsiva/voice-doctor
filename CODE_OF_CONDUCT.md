# Code of conduct

voice-doctor follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and keep other people's data out of public threads. Harassment, personal attacks and sharing someone's private information aren't tolerated.

Report problems privately through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/dakshinsiva/voice-doctor/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
